# Nabu

> Nabu is an AI-powered customs clearance platform that automates international trade compliance end to end. It collects shipment data from emails, PDFs, Excel files, databases, and TMS records, prepares complete compliant customs files, and generates pre-filled import/export declarations ready for submission in standard customs software. Nabu targets customs brokers and declaration teams. Backed by Techstars. No public developer API, documentation, or SDKs are published at this time.
